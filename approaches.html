What It Is
1 · FrameworkUse established frameworks to integrate AI into existing processes.
2 · HierarchicalTop-down transformation driven by strategy and organizational goals.
3 · PragmaticBottom-up, use-case-driven adoption based on real pain points.
4 · Governance & TrustRisk-based AI transformation that embeds governance, compliance and responsible AI controls across the lifecycle.
How It Works
1 · FrameworkMap AI to processes, align with governance and controls, extend current workflows.
2 · HierarchicalSet strategic objectives, cascade initiatives, align roles, KPIs and resources.
3 · PragmaticIdentify high-impact opportunities, experiment quickly, scale what works.
4 · Governance & TrustClassify AI use cases by risk, define controls, assign accountability, validate models/data, and monitor continuously.
Best For
1 · FrameworkRegulated environments, mature processes, strict compliance.
2 · HierarchicalCoordinated transformation, cross-functional alignment, large organizations.
3 · PragmaticFast-moving teams, early adoption, innovation-driven environments.
4 · Governance & TrustRegulated industries, enterprise-wide AI rollouts, high-impact decisions, and multi-region organizations.
Strength
1 · FrameworkPredictable, controlled, scalable.
2 · HierarchicalClear direction, alignment, accountability.
3 · PragmaticFast results, high engagement, tangible impact.
4 · Governance & TrustBuilds trust, reduces legal and reputational exposure, improves auditability, and enables safer scale.
Limitation
1 · FrameworkSlower to adapt, may limit experimentation.
2 · HierarchicalRisk of misalignment with real operational needs.
3 · PragmaticCan become fragmented without coordination.
4 · Governance & TrustCan slow early experimentation if controls become too heavy or too centralized.
Framework Alignment
1 · FrameworkPMBOK Maps to PMBOK Predictive (Waterfall) lifecycle. Use when scope, requirements and governance constraints are well-defined upfront. Ideal for regulated environments where change control is mandatory.
2 · HierarchicalITIL Aligns to ITIL Service Strategy practice - top-down definition of service value, portfolio decisions and investment prioritization cascade through the organization.
3 · PragmaticPMBOK Maps to PMBOK Adaptive (Agile) lifecycle. Use when requirements emerge through iteration and speed of learning matters more than upfront control. Ideal for Generative AI pilots and emerging use cases.
4 · Governance & TrustNIST / ISO Maps to NIST AI RMF (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage) and ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management system requirements).

PMBOK TAILORING PRINCIPLE PMBOK

PMBOK recommends tailoring the delivery approach to the project context. Combine all three approaches:

  • Pragmatic to discover opportunities → Adaptive (Agile)
  • Hierarchical to align efforts → Incremental
  • Framework to standardize and scale → Predictive (Waterfall)

The key question: What is the uncertainty level of this initiative? High uncertainty → lean Adaptive. Low uncertainty, high compliance → lean Predictive.

AI TRANSFORMATION IS MOST EFFECTIVE WHEN EXPERIMENTATION, DIRECTION, STRUCTURE, AND TRUST EVOLVE TOGETHER.
