What is an AI+ Organization?

An AI+ Organization is one that has embedded artificial intelligence not just as a tool but as a core operating capability. transforming how teams make decisions, create value, and continuously improve. This playbook provides a practical, opinionated roadmap for that transformation.

Use the sections in the left sidebar to navigate each layer of the model. Start with the Framework for a high-level view, then drill into Detailed Steps and the Workflow for execution guidance.

  • Understand your current AI maturity baseline
  • Align leadership, culture, and technology investments
  • Build repeatable AI delivery workflows at scale
  • Measure progress across well-defined maturity levels
  • Choose the right transformation approach for your context
