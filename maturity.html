CMMI Maturity Mapping Detail

CMMI Five Levels mapped to the AI+ Playbook's Three Levels:

  • CMMI Level 1 (Initial) → Playbook Level 1 (Adoption): Early, ad-hoc processes. Renamed here as "Controlled Discovery" to signal intentionality—exploration that is deliberate and tracked, not random.
  • CMMI Levels 2-3 (Managed & Defined) → Playbook Level 2 (Integration): Processes are standardized, documented, measured against baselines. Teams follow defined practices with measurable predictability.
  • CMMI Levels 4-5 (Quantitatively Managed & Optimizing) → Playbook Level 3 (Transformation): Continuous optimization driven by quantitative data. Processes improve themselves based on real-time feedback and experimentation.

Key insight: The playbook's "Level 1: Adoption" is NOT the same as CMMI's chaotic "Initial" state. It is controlled and deliberate, with clear intent and measurement. This distinction prevents the organization from mistaking exploration for lack of rigor.

Maturity is a journey, not a destination. Continue to iterate, measure, learn and raise the bar.
