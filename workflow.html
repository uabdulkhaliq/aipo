AI+ ORGANIZATION TRANSFORMATION WORKFLOW

A practical, repeatable framework to identify, build and scale AI initiatives that deliver measurable business impact.

Step 1 of 6
1
VALUE (WHY)
Define the core outcomes the organization wants to achieve with AI.
What Happens
  • Define strategic outcomes for AI (efficiency, quality, capability)
  • Align on business priorities and success metrics
  • Establish North Star goals and target impact
  • Get leadership alignment and commitment
Who Is Involved
  • Executive Leadership
  • Strategy / Planning
  • Finance / Business Control
ITIL SVS PMBOK
Output Clear value targets and success metrics
2
USE CASES (WHERE)
Standardized ways AI is applied across the organization.
What Happens
  • Gather bottom-up pain points and top-down opportunities
  • Classify use cases: Automation, Augmentation, Generation, Prediction, Optimization
  • Evaluate and score (impact, effort, risk, feasibility)
  • Prioritize top use cases (3–10 to start)
Who Is Involved
  • Business Leaders
  • Operations Teams
  • Product Owners / Managers
  • AI / Tech Explorer
Output Prioritized use case portfolio with expected value
3
OPERATING MODEL (HOW)
Define how humans and AI work together in real workflows.
What Happens
  • Choose interaction model:
    – Assistive (Human in Control)
    – Co-Pilot (AI + Human)
    – Autonomous (AI in Control)
  • Redesign end-to-end workflow: what is eliminated, automated, and remains human
  • Define decision ownership, approvals and exception handling
Who Is Involved
  • Process Owners
  • Department Leads
  • Architects / System Designers
  • End Users
Output Future-state workflow and operating model
4
FOUNDATION (WHAT ENABLES IT)
The essential data, tools and technology that make AI possible.
What Happens
  • Identify required data sources (structured & unstructured)
  • Assess data quality, accessibility, permissions and readiness
  • Select tools / models (AI/ML, LLMs, automation, etc.)
  • Design integrations (APIs, systems, platforms)
  • Build minimal viable foundation (avoid over-engineering)
Who Is Involved
  • Data Teams
  • IT / Engineering
  • Security Teams
  • Architects
Output Working technical foundation (data, tools, integrations)
5
GOVERNANCE (CONTROL & TRUST)
Ensure responsible, ethical and compliant use of AI across the organization.
What Happens
  • Define AI usage policies and guardrails (allowed vs restricted)
  • Implement privacy, security and access controls
  • Establish risk, bias and accuracy checks
  • Set up monitoring, logging and auditability
  • Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
Who Is Involved
  • Security / Compliance
  • Legal / Risk
  • IT Governance
  • Leadership (risk tolerance)

Output Safe, compliant and governed AI environment
6
EXECUTION (HOW IT HAPPENS)
Deliver AI solutions, embed in workflows and drive adoption.
What Happens
  • Assign roles: Explorer, Operator, Architect, Orchestrator
  • Build → Test → Deploy in short, iterative cycles
  • Embed solution into workflow and systems
  • Train users and drive adoption
  • Continuously improve based on feedback
Who Is Involved
  • Engineering / IT
  • Business Users
  • Transformation Team
  • Change Management
Output Live AI-enabled processes in use by the business

MEASURE IMPACT (VALIDATION) – ENCOMPASSES ALL STEPS

Measure outcomes, validate value and drive continuous improvement.

ITIL 7-Step Improvement PMBOK
What We Measure
Efficiency
  • Time saved
  • Cost reduction
  • Productivity gains
Quality
  • Error reduction
  • Accuracy improvement
  • Consistency & reliability
Capability
  • New outputs
  • Faster decisions
  • Innovation / new value
How We Measure
  • Establish baseline before implementation
  • Define KPIs for each use case
  • Track quantitative and qualitative outcomes
  • Compare against targets and benchmarks
  • Capture insights and lessons learned
Decisions After Measure
IMPROVE & ITERATE
Refine the solution, workflow or model and run the loop again.
SCALE & EXPAND
Extend to more users, processes or business areas.
STOP / REMOVE
Discontinue if no longer aligned to value or impact.
ITIL 7-Step Improvement Process
  1. Identify the improvement strategy
  2. Define what you will measure
  3. Gather the data
  4. Process the data
  5. Analyze the data
  6. Present and use the information
  7. Implement improvement

This loop maps directly to the “Decisions After Measure” cycle above—driving continual organizational learning and AI maturity beyond the initial deployment.

Continuous Loop: Every use case goes through this end-to-end cycle to drive greater value, maturity and organizational transformation.
