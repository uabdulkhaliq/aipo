AI+ ORGANIZATION FRAMEWORK
1
VALUE (WHY)
Defines the core outcomes the organization wants to achieve with AI.
Framework alignment ITIL SVS PMBOK
Efficiency
Do things faster and at lower cost. Reduce time, manual effort and operational spend.
Quality
Do things better with fewer errors. Improve accuracy, consistency and reliability.
Capability
Enable new outcomes and possibilities. Create new products, services and business models.
2
USE CASES (WHERE)
Standardized ways AI is applied across the organization.
Framework alignment PMBOK
Automation
Replaces repetitive, rule-based tasks end-to-end — reducing manual effort and eliminating error-prone handoffs.
Augmentation
Assists humans to work faster, more accurately, and with greater confidence — keeping people in control.
Generation
Creates new content, code, designs, or ideas on demand — dramatically compressing the time from brief to output.
Prediction
Forecasts outcomes, trends, and behaviors from historical data — enabling proactive decisions rather than reactive ones.
Optimization
Recommends the best actions or allocation of resources — continuously improving results across complex trade-offs.
3
OPERATING MODEL (HOW)
Defines how humans and AI work together in real workflows.
Framework alignment ITIL 4 Dimensions
Assistive
Human in Control
AI provides recommendations or information. Humans make decisions and take action.
Co-Pilot
AI + Human Collaboration
AI and humans collaborate throughout the process. Decisions are shared.
Autonomous
AI in Control, Human Oversees
AI executes tasks or decisions independently. Humans oversee, intervene when needed.
4
FOUNDATION
(WHAT ENABLES IT)
The essential data, tools and technology that make AI possible.
Framework alignment ITIL Design & Transition
Data
High quality, accessible and governed data (structured, unstructured and tacit knowledge).
Tools / Models
AI/ML models, LLMs, platforms and automation tools.
Integration
APIs, systems, workflows and infrastructure integration that enable AI to work at scale.
5
GOVERNANCE
(CONTROL & TRUST)
Ensures responsible, ethical and compliant use of AI across the organization.
Framework alignment NIST AI RMF COBIT PMBOK
Privacy & Security
Protect data, ensure access controls and maintain security.
Risk (Bias & Accuracy)
Identify and mitigate bias, inaccuracies and risks.
Compliance
Meet regulatory and legal obligations.
Auditability & Control
Ensure transparency, traceability and human control.
6
EXECUTION
(HOW IT HAPPENS)
How the organization delivers AI transformation and realizes value.
Framework alignment PMBOK Tailoring ITIL Continual Improvement
Approach
Choose the right approach: Framework-driven, Hierarchical (top-down) or Pragmatic (bottom-up).
Roles
Explorer, Operator, Architect and Orchestrator working together.
Prioritization
Focus on high impact use cases based on impact vs effort.
Adoption
Build capability, drive training and manage change for lasting adoption.
CROSS-CUTTING OVERLAYS – APPLY TO ALL 6 AREAS
MATURITY MODEL (HOW FAR) CMMI
Adoption
Early experimentation, isolated usage, learning phase.
Integration
AI embedded into workflows and processes across teams.
Transformation
Workflows are redesigned or eliminated. AI drives business transformation.
MEASUREMENT MODEL (WHAT IMPROVES)
Efficiency
Time saved, cost reduction, throughput increase.
Quality
Error reduction, consistency, rework reduction.
Capability
New outputs, faster decisions, scalability and innovation.
The Rule: Every AI initiative must define what improves, by how much, and how it is measured.
